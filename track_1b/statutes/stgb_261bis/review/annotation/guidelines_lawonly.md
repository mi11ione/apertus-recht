# Task (law-only annotation)

For each scenario, decide what a Swiss criminal court would conclude under Art. 261bis StGB/CP only, assuming the facts
stated are proven and nothing else is known. Use ONLY the legal sources in law.md (the article in its versions before
and since 1 July 2020, Art. 2 StGB on temporal scope, the footnotes on entry into force, and the Regeste of the leading
case BGE 130 IV 111). No summary of the rules is given on purpose: apply the sources yourself.

Labels: `convict` (every element met), `acquit` (at least one element clearly not met), `open` (no element clearly fails,
but whether one is met depends on facts the scenario leaves out). Deciding element for acquit/open: `setting`,
`characteristic`, `act_date`, `act`, or `other`; `none` for convict. Reason: at most 25 words, English. Note: start with
`WORDING:` if the text itself is defective.
